# Makhìa — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the game Makhìa ("the game") uses, why, and what rights you have. It is written to be short: the game has no accounts of its own, no servers of its own that keep your data, no advertising and no analytics.

## 1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Luigi Pennisi (Italy), independent developer of the game. Contact for any privacy question or request: <support@luigipennisi.dev>.

## 2. What data the game uses

**Steam ID, Steam name, avatar, friends list, online status**  
Why: Sign-in, finding friends, lobbies, invites, matchmaking  
Where it goes: Read from Steam while the game runs; we do not store it  
Legal basis (GDPR art. 6): Performance of the contract (art. 6.1.b): the game cannot work online without it

**Network address (IP) and connection data (ping, route)**  
Why: Connecting you to the other player in a match  
Where it goes: Your opponent's PC, or Valve's relay servers (Steam Datagram Relay) when a direct connection is not possible  
Legal basis (GDPR art. 6): Performance of the contract (art. 6.1.b)

**Voice**  
Why: Voice chat with the players in your party or match  
Where it goes: Sent in real time to those players only, while you hold the push-to-talk key; never recorded or stored  
Legal basis (GDPR art. 6): Performance of the contract (art. 6.1.b); you choose when to speak and can mute anyone

**Game status ("In a duel", "In the lobby")**  
Why: Discord Rich Presence, if Discord is running on your PC  
Where it goes: Your local Discord app, which shows it to your Discord friends  
Legal basis (GDPR art. 6): Legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f); turn it off in Discord's Activity Privacy settings

**Crash reports: crash dump, call stack, game log (may contain Steam names and IDs), hardware and OS information, IP address, random device identifiers set by Unreal Engine (and your Epic Games account ID, if the Epic Games Launcher is signed in on your PC); file paths may contain your Windows user name**  
Why: Finding and fixing crashes  
Where it goes: BugSplat (USA)  
Legal basis (GDPR art. 6): Consent (art. 6.1.a): after a crash the game asks you each time; nothing is sent unless you press Send, and you can untick the game log

**Settings, key bindings, saves, career progress**  
Why: Remembering your preferences and progress  
Where it goes: Only on your PC (and Steam Cloud, if enabled)  
Legal basis (GDPR art. 6): Performance of the contract (art. 6.1.b)

The game is free and has no purchases. It does not collect your real name, e-mail, payment data or location.

## 3. Third parties

These companies process data as independent controllers or as our processors, under their own policies:

- Valve Corporation (Steam: sign-in, friends, networking, leaderboards, Steam Cloud) — USA — <https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/>

- Discord Inc. (Rich Presence, only if you use Discord) — USA — <https://discord.com/privacy>

- BugSplat, LLC (crash reports, only if you choose to send one) — USA — <https://docs.bugsplat.com/introduction/production/security-privacy-and-compliance/privacy-policy>

Some of them are in the United States. Valve and Discord receive data from you as independent controllers and handle transfers under their own policies. For BugSplat, which processes crash reports on our behalf, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in its data processing agreement.

Other players in your match see your Steam name and avatar, hear your voice when you speak, and may see your IP address when the connection is direct.

## 4. How long we keep data

- Steam, network, voice and Discord data: not kept by us; used only while the game runs.

- Crash reports: 30 days, then deleted automatically.

- E-mails to support: as long as needed to answer, at most 2 years.

## 5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Consent to a crash report is given for that report only; you can ask us to delete a report you sent. Write to <support@luigipennisi.dev>; we answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (https://www.garanteprivacy.it), or to the authority of the country where you live.

## 6. Children

Steam accounts require a minimum age set by Valve. The game is not directed at children under 13. If you are younger than the age of digital consent in your country (14 in Italy), ask a parent or guardian before sending a crash report.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Important changes are also announced on the game's Steam page.
